Trigonometric Functions: Sine and Cosine

Unit Circle Definition
Point at \((x,y)\), radius \(r=1\)
$$\sin \theta=\frac{y}{r}=y$$ $$\cos \theta=\frac{x}{r}=x$$ Equivalently, the point is at \((\cos \theta, \sin \theta)\).

Basic Identities
Pythagorean identity $$\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$$
Parity $$\sin(-\theta)=-\sin\theta$$ $$\cos(-\theta)=\cos\theta$$
Reflections $$\sin(\theta+\pi)=-\sin\theta$$ $$\cos(\theta+\pi)=-\cos\theta$$
Shift by quarter period $$\sin(\theta+\pi/2)=\cos\theta$$ $$\cos(\theta+\pi/2)=-\sin\theta$$
General Sine Function
General sine function is a family of functions: $$f(x)=a\sin(bx+c)+d$$
\(a\) is the amplitude
\(b\) controls the period
\(c\) controls horizontal shift
\(d\) controls vertical shift
